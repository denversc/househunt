# Clue #1: Mailbox Setup

* **Where to hide the card:** In the **Mailbox** on the front porch.
* **What to write on the physical card:**
  > *"I have sharp eyes and soar high in the sky. I am a bird of prey. What am I? (5 letters)"*
* **The answer to type into the app:** **`EAGLE`** (the check is case-insensitive, so both `eagle` and `EAGLE` will work).
